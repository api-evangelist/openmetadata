Membership Deepens Commitment to Vendor-Neutral Semantic Standards and Community-Driven Innovation SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. — March 26, 2026 — Collate Inc. , the company behind the open source OpenMetadata project, today announced it has joined the Linux Foundation as a Silver Member to help drive the industry shift towards open semantic intelligence.
